\section{Handling Large Data Sets}
    Sometimes (most of the time),  being a Data Scientist requires us to deal with massive data sets that involve cleaning, organizing, and restructuring what were given. 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Most computers do not have enough RAM to open large data sets
        \item You may be able to split data into more manageable chunks. 
        \item Can use Resilient Distributed Datasets (RDDs)
        \item Coding in a Structured Query Language (SQL) can help subset and merge moderately sized datasets
    \end{itemize}
